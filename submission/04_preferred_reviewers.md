# Preferred reviewers

For the submission form of *Journal of Microbiological Methods*. Three names, one
alternate. Every affiliation and every email below was read from a source the
search actually opened — a printed correspondence line or an official
institutional page — and the source is named beside it. Nothing here is inferred
from a name pattern.

## The rule they were chosen under

The author's instruction was unusual and it is the point of the list: **active in
tuberculosis, and with no publication of their own on this paper's method.**
Disqualifying on the method side meant any paper on limits of detection or
quantification, left-censored or below-limit data, Tobit / Turnbull / Beal
estimation, MDK or the Balaban–Brauner tolerance framework, population or
semi-mechanistic PK/PD kill modelling, hollow-fibre model fitting, meta-analysis
of published time-kill data, or CFU enumeration methodology. Also disqualifying:
co-authorship with any author of a deposit this paper reanalyses, and
co-authorship on the van Wijk 2026 relapse preprint.

Twenty-nine candidates were assessed across five TB sub-territories; nine were
verified in full and five of those were disqualified, three at verification and
two by a second agent whose only brief was to disqualify them. What follows is
what survived both.

## 1. Marcus A. Horwitz, MD

**Distinguished Professor of Medicine**, Division of Infectious Diseases,
Department of Medicine, and Department of Microbiology, Immunology & Molecular
Genetics, David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA, CHS 32-150, 10833 Le Conte
Avenue, Los Angeles, California 90095-1688, USA

**mhorwitz@mednet.ucla.edu** — printed correspondence line of Lee BY *et al.*,
*J Infect Dis* 2025;232(3):e442–e447 (PMID 40498595), read at PMC12455299;
corroborated in Jia Q *et al.*, *Commun Biol* 2022;5:1388 (PMID 36539517).

**Why him.** He runs one of the leading mouse and guinea-pig TB chemotherapy
laboratories, and his regimens are scored on exactly the endpoints this paper is
about: lung CFU burden, time to lung sterilisation, and relapse-free cure. He is
therefore placed to judge whether a detection floor really does bound those
endpoints, and what that means for a treatment-shortening claim.

**Why he is not a competitor.** Five literal PubMed searches under his name
across the excluded topics returned nothing — no limit-of-detection or censoring
paper, no MDK or tolerance-metric paper, no population PK/PD or hollow-fibre
fitting, no CFU-enumeration methodology. The one thing that looks adjacent, his
laboratory's Parabolic Response Surface platform for optimising drug
combinations, is a search over a response surface with a single efficacy readout;
its own full text states that no PK/PD or NONMEM-type analysis was performed.

## 2. Tehmina Mustafa, MBBS, PhD

**Professor**, Centre for International Health, Department of Global Public
Health and Primary Care, Faculty of Medicine, University of Bergen, Bergen,
Norway; leader of its Tuberculosis Research Group; consultant, Department of
Thoracic Medicine, Haukeland University Hospital, Bergen

**tehmina.mustafa@uib.no** — official University of Bergen employee page,
fetched; corroborated in the correspondence lines of PMID 39623309, PMID
39413665 and PMID 39343776.

**Why her.** She has spent two decades on the problem this paper turns on:
detecting *M. tuberculosis* when there is almost nothing there to detect. The
MPT64 antigen test her group built exists because culture and Xpert miss
paucibacillary and extrapulmonary disease — which is the clinical form of the
argument that below the floor is not the same as nothing. She can judge the
claim on microbiological and clinical grounds at once.

**Why she is not a competitor.** No paper of hers touches censoring statistics,
MDK or tolerance metrics, or kill-curve modelling. A dedicated disqualification
pass over her full Bergen-affiliated record found nothing.

## 3. Norbert O. Ndjeka, MD

**Director, TB Control and Management**, National Department of Health, Republic
of South Africa, Pretoria; Department of Medicine, Faculty of Health Sciences,
University of Cape Town; School of Nursing and Public Health, University of
KwaZulu-Natal, Durban

**norbert.ndjeka@health.gov.za** — correspondence line of Ndjeka N *et al.*,
*Lancet Infect Dis* 2022;22(7):1042–1051, read in full at PMC9217754; the same
address is the electronic address on PMID 35512718 and on PMID 36966798.

**Why him.** He directs the national TB programme of the country with one of the
world's largest drug-resistant burdens, and he is first or corresponding author
on the cohort work behind the short all-oral bedaquiline regimens. The paper's
closing claim is a clinical one — that a tolerance call published without its
starting density and its assay floor cannot be interpreted — and he is the person
who acts on culture-based cure definitions at national scale.

**Why he is not a competitor.** His complete 78-record PubMed corpus was
retrieved and scanned by the disqualification pass: no censoring, no MDK, no
pharmacometrics, no enumeration methodology, and no co-authorship with any
deposit author.
