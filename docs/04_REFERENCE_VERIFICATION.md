### Severity notes

- **Fabricated-looking entries (real author strings welded onto invented titles/venues):** #5, #11, #12, #13, #19, #22, #24. In each of these the author list corresponds exactly (or near-exactly) to a genuine paper, but the title and/or journal, volume and pages are wrong — the signature pattern of LLM-generated references. These require full replacement, not a field correction.
- **Wholly unlocatable:** #14 and #20. No PubMed record matches either as printed, and the cited volume/page slots are occupied by unrelated articles or do not exist.
- **Citation used for an unrelated claim:** #24 — the underlying paper is real, but it concerns hydrogen-peroxide-mediated interspecies killing between *S. pneumoniae* and *S. aureus*, and has nothing to do with persister-targeting compounds. It should be removed from that argument rather than merely re-formatted.
- **Minor field errors only:** #3 (issue, title, and a wrong author), #10 (issue), #26 (title wording).
